Capabilities

Everything you need to
analyse the system

From real-time Canvas rendering to CSV exports and scenario comparisons — the full toolkit for your report.

🏭
Live Warehouse Canvas
40×28 cell grid with shelves, packing stations, chargers, and obstacles rendered in real-time at 30 fps.
🤖
Multi-Agent Robots
Up to 50 autonomous robots with priority-based decision logic: battery → deliver → fetch.
🔋
Battery Management
Each robot independently manages its battery. Emergent charging queues form with large fleets.
🚦
Collision Avoidance
Cone-of-vision detection (1.5 radius, 60° half-angle) with randomised avoidance turn — mirrors NetLogo in-cone.
🔥
Congestion Heatmap
Toggle a live heat overlay showing which aisles are bottlenecks. Heat decays at 5% per tick.
📈
Real-time Charts
Throughput, battery levels, robot states, and collision counts plotted live with Chart.js.
🎬
Speed Control
Run at 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 5×, or 10× speed. Step through one tick at a time for debugging.
🔍
Robot Inspector
Click any robot to inspect its state, battery, task count, collision history, and current target.
📦
Scenario Presets
One-click Scenario 1/2/3 buttons pre-configure exactly the settings from your assessment brief.
💾
Data Export
Export simulation data as CSV, PNG snapshot of the canvas, or full JSON session for your report.
📊
Scenario Comparison
Auto-run all three scenarios and generate a side-by-side comparison table on the Report page.
⌨️
Keyboard Shortcuts
Space = play/pause, R = reset, S = step, H = heatmap, T = trails, 1/2/3 = scenarios.
